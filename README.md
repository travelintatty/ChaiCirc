# ChaiCirc
**Life Across the Generations**

ChaiCirc is a family-care concept for connecting goals, events, and health across generations.

## Demo for reviewers

**Start here:** https://chaicirc.stratplanact.com/preview-health/

Sample pages:
- [Goals — StratPlanAct Treatment sample](https://chaicirc.stratplanact.com/preview-health/goals-sample.html)
- [Events — Family birthdays and yahrzeits](https://chaicirc.stratplanact.com/preview-health/birthdays-yahrzeits.html)

## What to explore
- **Goals:** a sample strategy/treatment framework.
- **Events:** family dates with birthday and yahrzeit examples.
- **Health:** a visual prototype with patient, caregiver, and family-member paths.
- **Information request:** the landing page includes an email form for people who want information about ChaiCirc.

## Important demo notes
- This is a demonstration prototype, not a secure medical-records service.
- Health screens use illustrative/synthetic information. Do not enter real medical or other sensitive personal information.
- Some sections are still planned for future versions; the demo does not represent a finished production service.
